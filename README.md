# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze employee attrition, retention, salary, job satisfaction, demographics, departments, job roles, and business travel.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform HR data into meaningful visual insights and understand employee attrition patterns and workforce characteristics.

## 📌 Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|-----|-----:|
| Total Employees | 1.48K |
| Attrition Count | 238 |
| Attrition Rate | 16.08% |
| Retention Rate | 83.92% |
| Average Monthly Income | 6.50K |
| Average Job Satisfaction | 2.73 |

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard analyzes:

- Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Salary Slab
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition by Education
- Attrition by Marital Status
- Attrition by Business Travel
- Attrition by Years at Company
- Years at Company vs Monthly Income
- Employee Attrition and Retention

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The organization has **1.48K employees** with **238 attrition cases**.
- The overall **attrition rate is 16.08%**, while the **retention rate is 83.92%**.
- The **26–35 age group** has the highest attrition count with **116 cases**.
- The **18–25 age group** has the highest displayed attrition rate at **35.77%**.
- Employees earning **up to 5K** account for the highest number of attrition cases with **163 cases**.
- **Research & Development** has the highest department-level attrition with **133 cases**, followed by Sales with **93 cases**.
- Employees in the **Travel_Rarely** category account for the highest number of attrition cases with **157 cases**.
- Among attrition cases, **Single employees account for 50.42%**, followed by Married employees at **35.29%**.
- Attrition is more concentrated among employees with **shorter tenure**, while it decreases considerably at higher years of service.
